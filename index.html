Assistant Professor
School of Computing and Information Systems

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor at Singapore Management University. My research centers on multimodality and forecasting. I lead LMF: Lab for Multimodality and Forecasting, where we connect multimodal modeling with forecasting across scales.

Before joining SMU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the NExT++ Research Center, National University of Singapore, supervised by Prof. Tat-Seng Chua. I also worked with Prof. Chang Ee-Chien and Prof. Liang Zhenkai at the National Cybersecurity R&D Lab. I received my PhD from NUS in 2022, supervised by Prof. Chua.

I am interested in discovering and formulating new problems, and in developing data mining, information retrieval, and multimedia methods with practical impact.
