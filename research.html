Multimodality
& forecasting.

At the Lab for Multimodality and Forecasting, we study how to understand and combine heterogeneous information, and how to anticipate what happens next—from global events to individual behavior.

DIRECTION 01

Multimodality

We study multimodal understanding and fusion, together with compositional and combinatorial modeling, across text, images, audio, graphs, and symbolic data. Our methods capture relationships both within and across modalities, with applications in bundle construction, recommender systems, and other structured decision problems.

  • Multimodal understanding and fusion
  • Compositional and combinatorial modeling
  • Bundle construction and recommender systems
Related publications →
DIRECTION 02

Forecasting

We develop forecasting methods across scales. At the macro level, we study financial forecasting and geopolitical event forecasting. At the micro level, we model user behavior in recommender systems and predict cyber attack sequences.

  • Macro-level: financial markets and geopolitical events
  • Micro-level: user behavior and recommendation
  • Micro-level: cyber attack sequence prediction
