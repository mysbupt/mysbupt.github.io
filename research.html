Fundamental Technologies

MULTIMODALITY

Multimodality

We investigate multimodal understanding, fusion, and compositional and combinatorial modeling across text, images, audio, graphs, and symbolic data.

Understanding and Fusion

Learning representations of heterogeneous information, understanding the relationships between modalities, and combining complementary signals.

Compositional and Combinatorial Modeling

Modeling how individual elements combine into coherent structures and sets, with applications in bundle construction, recommender systems, and related tasks.

FORECASTING

Forecasting

We aim to anticipate future events and behavior at both macro and micro scales.

Macro-level Forecasting

Financial forecasting and geopolitical event forecasting: reasoning about markets, economies, and evolving events in the wider world.

Micro-level Forecasting

User behavior forecasting in recommender systems and cyber attack sequence prediction: modeling how individual actions and sequences unfold over time.
