What we study

01

Multimodality

Understanding and fusing text, images, audio, graphs, and symbolic data; modeling how their elements compose and combine.

Explore this direction →
02

Forecasting

Anticipating macro-level events and micro-level behavior, from financial markets and geopolitics to users and cyber attacks.

Explore this direction →

Where we apply it

Fashion

Fashion understanding, personalized styling, outfit and wardrobe generation, and trend forecasting.

Finance

Financial forecasting, explainable stock prediction, time-series reasoning, and financial analysis.

Cybersecurity

Cyber threat intelligence, attack graph construction, and cyber attack sequence prediction.

Social Events

Understanding and forecasting social and geopolitical events through graph modeling, open-ended hypothesis generation, and multi-expert reasoning.

Music

Connecting symbolic music and audio with language models for music understanding and personalization.
